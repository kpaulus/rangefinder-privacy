PinRead Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

PinRead is a golf rangefinder app. It works on your phone: it doesn't have accounts, it doesn't ask for your name or email, and it doesn't track you. This page explains exactly what the app does with the camera, your location, and — only if you turn it on — your photos.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera. PinRead takes a photo of the flagstick to measure the distance to the pin. The photo stays on your phone unless you turn on sharing (below).
  • Location and heading (while you're using the app). Your position is used to project the pin's position and to look up the course, hole, and green edges. Location is not used in the background.
  • Motion. The phone's pitch is measured to correct the slant-to-ground distance calculation.

Services the app calls to work

Like any app that answers "what course am I on" and "which way is the wind", PinRead sends your approximate position to services that answer those questions. These calls carry only the coordinates of the shot at hand:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhat it returns
Apple WeatherKitYour coordinates Current weather, for shot context
Open-Meteo (used only if WeatherKit fails) Your coordinatesCurrent weather
OpenStreetMapYour coordinates The course, hole, and green geometry

These services receive nothing else — no photos, no identifiers, and nothing that identifies your device beyond the app name and version every app sends with a web request.

Optional capture sharing (off by default)

There is one optional feature: Share captures to improve the model, in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on, and turning it on is the whole consent — nothing is written for upload, let alone sent, until then.

When sharing is on, each capture you take is uploaded to help train and improve the distance model. Uploads use any available connection, including cellular data, unless you turn on Upload on Wi-Fi only in Settings. Each capture includes:

  • the photo itself, and small cropped images the app derived from it;
  • the location, heading, and motion sensor values at the moment of the shot, plus the time;
  • the course, hole, and app version, and your phone model;
  • an anonymous install ID — a random ID stored on your phone, which is never sent anywhere unless sharing is on. It is not tied to your Apple ID, your hardware, or anything else, and it resets if you reinstall the app. It exists so that captures from one install can be reviewed as a group; it cannot be used to contact you or find you.

What the photo does not contain: before a capture is ever written for upload, the app removes location (GPS) metadata and device serial/owner fields from the photo's EXIF metadata, and a second check refuses to upload any photo in which those fields somehow survived. The location facts above travel in the capture's data file because they are exactly what makes the capture useful for training — the photo itself carries none.

Every capture uploads while sharing is on, wherever it was taken, so only turn sharing on while you are using the app for golf. If you turn sharing on and then off, no new captures are sent from that point on.

"Mark pin" uploads a position, not a photo. The Mark pin button — on the capture screen and the review screen — records where the hole's pin actually stands: you stand at the cup, tap, and the app averages a few seconds of GPS fixes. A mark uploads only while sharing is on, the same consent as captures, and contains the averaged position with its reported accuracy, the individual fixes it was averaged from, and the course and hole shown on screen at the time. It takes no photo and uploads no image, and it never uploads when sharing is off.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no name, email, or contact info.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no cross-app tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No background location: PinRead reads your position only while you are using it.

Your controls

  • Turn sharing off any time in Settings → Data collection. Queued captures that have not yet sent can simply be deleted on-device.
  • Delete what's on your phone: captures live in the app's folder in the Files app, and are removed completely by deleting the app.
  • Delete what was uploaded: email the address below with roughly when and where you played and the captures will be located and deleted from the training set.
  • Reinstall to reset your install ID and end its association with any captures.

Data retention and security

Captures you share are stored in the developer's private cloud storage and reviewed by hand, by the developer, to label training data. They are kept until they are no longer useful for improving the model, or until you ask for them to be deleted. Uploads are write-only to that storage.

Children

PinRead is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Material changes to what is collected are additionally announced in the app's release notes.
